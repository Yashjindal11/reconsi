# Evaluation against known ground truth

Generated by `python benchmarks/evaluate.py` with ReconSI 0.1.0.dev0: 20,000 rows per dataset, seeds [0, 1, 2, 3, 4]. Each scenario injects a known corruption with `reconsi.synthetic.generate_reconciliation_pair` and checks whether ReconSI reports exactly what was injected. Precision/recall are averaged over seeds; boolean detections show the share of seeds in which the diagnosis was made.

These scenarios are unambiguous by construction (each corruption touches a disjoint set of rows, keys are clean, effects are well above detection limits), so they verify correctness rather than sensitivity. Where detection gets hard (small effects, few records) is measured in [research.md](research.md).

## Scenarios

- **clean**: Identical datasets: nothing should be reported.
- **missing_and_extra**: 2% of rows dropped from the right, 1% new rows added.
- **value_mismatches**: 1% of rows have a changed revenue, quantity or status.
- **duplicates**: 0.5% of keys duplicated on the right; reconciled with duplicate_strategy=first.
- **rounding_with_tolerance**: 2% of revenues rounded to whole units; an absolute tolerance of 0.5 should absorb them.
- **rounding_without_tolerance**: Same rounding with no tolerance: every rounded value must be reported.
- **timestamp_shift**: 0.5% of updated_at values shifted by +05:30 (a timezone error).
- **string_formatting**: 0.5% of customer names upper-cased and space-padded.
- **systematic_bias_segment**: Revenue 2.5% higher on the right for region West only, plus random value errors.
- **segment_missing**: 1% of rows missing from the right, all from channel=phone.
- **incident_window**: Revenue corrupted for 25% of orders on days 14-17 only.
- **grain_difference**: Order-level left vs a daily summary on the right with one day off by 100.

Notes: `systematic difference flagged` is expected to be 0% for `value_mismatches` (random-direction errors) and 100% for `systematic_bias_segment`. `spurious change points` (mean per run) for `incident_window`: 0.00.
